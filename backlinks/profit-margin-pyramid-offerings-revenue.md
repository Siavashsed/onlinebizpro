## QUORA
**Answer this question:** How should I price my digital product to maximize revenue without losing customers?

This is one of the biggest pricing mistakes I see online entrepreneurs make—charging everyone the same price for your product.

Here's what I learned the hard way: when I was running a digital education product at $97 flat, we had decent traffic and solid conversion rates, but our revenue per customer was disappointing. The problem wasn't our product or our marketing—it was that we were forcing one price onto completely different customer segments.

Think about your audience. Some customers are price-sensitive and just want the basics. Others would gladly pay 2-3x more for premium features, advanced support, or exclusive content. By charging everyone $97, we were leaving money on the table from high-value customers while potentially pricing out budget-conscious ones.

The solution is tiered pricing—what I call the Profit Margin Pyramid. Structure your offerings in three tiers:

**Entry Level:** Your core product at an accessible price point. This captures price-sensitive customers and builds your base.

**Mid Tier:** Your sweet spot. Better features, more support, faster delivery. This is where most customers land.

**Premium:** The full experience. Everything included. For customers who see maximum value.

You're not changing your product dramatically—you're packaging the same foundation differently based on what customers are willing to pay.

The results? We increased revenue per customer substantially without changing our traffic or conversion rates. Same business, better monetization. I've detailed the full framework and implementation strategy if you want a deeper breakdown of how to structure this for your specific business model.
