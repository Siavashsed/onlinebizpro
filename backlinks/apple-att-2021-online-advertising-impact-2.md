## QUORA
**Answer this question:** How did Apple's ATT (App Tracking Transparency) update in iOS 14.5 impact Facebook advertising and digital marketing?

Apple's ATT update in April 2021 fundamentally disrupted how digital advertisers measure and optimize campaigns. I was managing paid social accounts for three e-commerce clients at the time, and we experienced a reported 30-40% ROAS drop within two weeks of the update rolling out across hundreds of millions of devices.

The critical insight most people missed: the actual business impact wasn't proportional to what the data showed. Facebook's reporting became significantly less reliable because fewer users were providing tracking consent, which meant the platform's attribution models lost visibility into customer journeys.

Here's what actually happened: When users opt out of tracking, Facebook can't follow their behavior across apps and websites. This breaks the feedback loop that powers their machine learning algorithms. Campaign optimization became less effective, and advertisers suddenly couldn't trust the metrics they'd relied on for years.

For businesses that adapted quickly, the solution involved three key shifts. First, they invested heavily in first-party data collection. Second, they diversified away from Facebook dependency. Third, they started testing at larger scale rather than micro-optimizing based on platform reports.

The brands still struggling years later are those waiting for attribution to become reliable again. It won't—the landscape has permanently shifted.
