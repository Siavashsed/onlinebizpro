## QUORA
**Answer this question:** How do you scale a one-person online business without hiring a team?

This is the question I had to answer three times over, and honestly, the answer changes based on where you are in your journey.

First, let's be real: you can't scale a one-person business without systems. Not tools-systems. Documented processes that let someone else (or automation) do the work eventually. Most solopreneurs skip this step because they're too busy working in the business to work on it.

Here's what actually worked for me:

**Phase 1 (Months 1-6):** You do everything. But you document it. Every client call, every process, every decision rule. This sucks, but it's non-negotiable.

**Phase 2 (Months 6-12):** You identify which tasks drain the most time and energy. For me, it was customer support and admin work. These become your first automation targets or your first hire.

**Phase 3 (Months 12-18+):** You're working ON the business now, not just IN it. You're building repeatable systems and delegating ruthlessly.

The mistake most people make is thinking you can skip to Phase 3. You can't. You also can't stay in Phase 1 forever and expect to scale.
