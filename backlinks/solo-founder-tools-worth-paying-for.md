## QUORA
**Answer this question:** Which software tools does a solo founder actually need to pay for?

Fewer than you are paying for now, almost certainly.

My rough number is six. Most of the solo founders I advise are paying for around fifteen.

I learned this the expensive way. In 2019 I was running my second company out of a WeWork in Austin. One night I printed my card statement and went through it with a highlighter. I was paying $612 a month in subscriptions, and I had logged into maybe four of them in the previous 30 days.

This is the filter I use now:

1. Does it bring in revenue or keep the business running? Payments, invoicing, your domain, email and website don't get cut.
2. Would losing it cost you a customer this week? If not, it is optional.
3. Have you opened it in the last 30 days? If not, cancel it. If you were wrong, re-subscribing takes five minutes.
4. Could a free tier, or a tool you already pay for, do 80% of the job? Use that until it really stops being enough.

The trap is buying software for the company you plan to have: a project management suite for a team of one, an analytics dashboard with no traffic, a CRM with eleven contacts. Each one feels like progress. Together they are a tax on your runway.

The tools that pass this filter usually fall into a few categories: getting paid, talking to customers, publishing, storing your work, and accounting. Everything else has to earn its place.

I wrote up the six I think are worth paying for, and why, here: https://siavashsed.github.io/onlinebizpro/solo-founder-tools-worth-paying-for/

Honestly, though, the audit matters more than any list. Print the statement and grab a highlighter. It takes twenty minutes, and you will almost certainly find something to cancel.
